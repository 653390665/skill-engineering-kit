## Quality bar
A valid output must:

- be actionable;
- separate facts from assumptions;
- state boundaries;
- include next step;
- include tests or acceptance criteria when execution is requested.

## Structures
Choose from:

- brief;
- checklist;
- matrix;
- audit report;
- pressure-test brief;
- optimization roadmap;
- release gate;
- regression log.
